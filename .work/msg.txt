docs: let the surface carry the settings, in the surface's own place

The case record kept the first version of the settings design, which the user
rejected: a miniature preview inside the settings panel. What replaced it is
worth recording — the edit surface is the capture's own clone mounted over the
conversation, the chrome piece table is the single list of what can be
switched off, and a switched-off part stays in place, dimmed. Also notes the
four mechanics that bit: rebuild on a revision counter rather than on the
options object, cancel clicks inside the clone, mount the overlay on body, and
layer it above the dialog layer but below the popup layer.

The UI standard gains the same rule in general form, and a short paragraph on
where an edit surface belongs and how the piece list has to be shared.
